NOTES

(1) // tavolo: the table, and /a tavola: the plank. But when one must
set the table for meals, one says: mettere la tavola.

(2) In Italian, the gender of towns is always feminine: La Londra del
XVIII secolo: Eighteenth century London.

(3) Notice ecco (here, there) is always followed by the pronouns /o0,
la, li, ie, ne and should be written in one word: eccola, eccone,
eccoli, etc.

(4) Ho fatto il più presto possibile: | hurried as much as possible, but
also: Mi sono alzato presto stamattina: i got up early this
morning.

(5) Non ho molti soldi: | don't have much money. / soldi non mi
bastano mai: | never have enough money. / soldi is the most
common word for money, and is always plurali. Another word for
money is danaro (or denaro) but it's less frequently used. Non
ho molto denaro: | don't have much money. Denaro is always
singuiar.

‘ Preso: past participle of prendere; dato: dare;
chiesto: chiedere.
