(1) The following three adjectives indicating colours are always
invariable: b/u, rosa (pink) and viola (violet). Thus: un nastro rosa
(a pink ribbon).

(2) Here is the complete present tense of the verb riuscire (notice
how it is composed of r# and uscire, and the same order of
sequence of u and e applies): (io) riesco, (tu) riesci, (lui/lei)
riesce, (noi) riusciamo, (voi) riuscite, (loro) riescono. Notice that
one says: Riesce sempre a ottenere quello che vuole: He always
succeeds in getting what he wants. Don't forget that in Italian,
the verb arrivare can only be used in a sentence such as: Arrivo
alle sette: l'm arriving at seven.

(3) In Italian, it's not unusual nor impolite to address a youngish-
looking woman by calling her signorina!
