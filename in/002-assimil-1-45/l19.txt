NOTES

(1) A Amalfibecomes ad Amalfi: the dis added to make a connection
(as in the previous Carlos ed Isabella).

(2) Singuiar: archeologico (pronounced ahrrrkeholodjeecoh). Most
words with -co become —ci in the plural, thus simpatico (likeable,
nice) becomes simpatici, economico (economical): economici,
amico (friend): amici. But there are also those which become -
chi (pronounced kee): tedesco (german): tedeschi, franco (frank):
franchi.

The same applies for the words ending with -go such as psicologo
(psychologist): psicologi, chirurgo (surgeon): chirurghi.

(3) Sono: when you refer to a period which is riot finished, you must
use the present tense. Siamo qui da due settimane: we've been
here for two weeks.

speaking with deference, whether one is speaking to a man or to
a woman.
