A Room with a View of the Sea

1 — Sir, Madam, here is your room.

2 l'Il open the shutters immediately and show
you the view.
3 — Oh, how marvellous. Look dear, the sea is

NOTES

(1) Guarda! (look), Ascolta! (listen), Osserva! (observe): verbs of the
first group (infinitive in -are) end with an a in the imperative. All
the others end with an / (that is, the second person singular in
the indicative present is used for the imperative): Prendi quella
valigia!: Take this suitcase! Apri quella finestra! Open that
window! As for the plural, the second person plural in
the indicative present is used for all three groups: Guardate!
Prendete! Aprite!
